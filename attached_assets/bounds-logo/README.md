# Bounds logo starter set

## Primary idea

The broken ring represents a geographic boundary and a focused work session. Its opening points forward, while the central point represents protected attention.

## Colors

- Deep navy: `#14243A`
- Bounds amber: `#F5A623`
- Warm cream: `#FFF4DF`

## Files

- `bounds-app-store-icon-512.png`: Google Play listing icon, full-bleed 512 × 512 PNG.
- `bounds-app-store-icon.svg`: scalable source for the store icon.
- `bounds-mark-color.svg` / `.png`: transparent standalone color mark.
- `bounds-wordmark-dark.svg` / `.png`: for light backgrounds.
- `bounds-wordmark-light.svg` / `.png`: for dark backgrounds.
- `bounds-mark-monochrome-dark.svg` / `.png`: one-color mark for light backgrounds.
- `bounds-mark-monochrome-light.svg` / `.png`: one-color mark for dark backgrounds.

## Usage

- Do not add another rounded mask to the Google Play icon; the store applies its own shape.
- Keep clear space around the standalone mark equal to the center point’s diameter.
- Do not rotate, stretch, outline, or recolor individual pieces of the mark.
